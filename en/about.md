I am an incoming PhD student at [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC)](https://illinois.edu/), starting from Fall 2026, and will be advised by [Dr. Yue Guo](https://yueguo-50.github.io/). I'm currently having my postgraduate residency at [National Taiwan University Hospital (NTUH)](https://www.ntuh.gov.tw/ntuh/Index.action?l=en_US). In addition to my expertise in medicine, I studied computer science in the CS department in [National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU)](https://www.nycu.edu.tw/nycu/en/index). Having great interest in AI algorithms, my future aim is to improve healthcare system with artificial intelligence as well as designing digital software systems for personalized medicine.

To be specific, I am interested in but not limited to the following research directions:
1. **Generalizable Fairness**: How do we generalize our medical models across different institutions, ethnicities, and under conditions of restricted patient data? I aim to detect distribution shifts and develop new calibration techniques to improve worst-group performance.
2. **Human-centric Verification**: How do we build tools that are truly useful in a clinical workflow? I plan to design and translate multi-modal AI models into real clinical settings, conducting clinical trials or simulation studies to measure their impact on patients’ outcomes.
3. **Human-machine Concept Alignment**: Explainability cannot be just a heatmap. I aim to translate complex model activations into rich, narrative explanations for clinicians. I will also focus on validating whether these AI-generated concepts are new, meaningful strategies
beyond human minds or simply ‘clever’ shortcut-based illusions.
